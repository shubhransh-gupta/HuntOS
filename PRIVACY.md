# HuntOS Privacy Policy

## Local-First

HuntOS stores all data locally in your browser using IndexedDB:

- Resume and profile
- Job listings and match analysis
- Application history
- Hunt profiles and settings

HuntOS does **not** require an account. There is no server-side storage.

## AI Provider Data

When you configure an AI provider, selected content (resume text, job descriptions) may be sent to that provider for parsing and generation. This is controlled by you through Settings.

API keys are stored locally in IndexedDB and are never committed to version control.

## Notifications

Browser notifications are local only. No email infrastructure.

## Your Controls

- **Export all data** — download complete JSON export
- **Delete all local data** — wipe IndexedDB and reset app

## No Scraping

HuntOS does not bypass CAPTCHA, authentication, or anti-bot protections. Job sources use sample data, manual import, and permitted methods only.
